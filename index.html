Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you download the trainer.

Is the MECCHA CHAMELEON Trainer free?+
Yes — it's completely free and open source under the MIT license. You can download the latest build from gitscore.cc, and the source is available for anyone who wants to audit or build it themselves.
Will it work on Windows 10 and Windows 11?+
The trainer is built for 64-bit Windows 10 (version 1903 and newer) and all Windows 11 builds. It runs alongside the game without any additional runtime or .NET installation.
Can I use it in online or co-op modes?+
No. The trainer is designed for single-player only. Using it in any online session would be unfair to other players and could get your account restricted. Keep it to your own save file.
How do I update or uninstall it?+
Updates are simple: download the newer archive from gitscore.cc and replace the old folder. To uninstall, just delete the trainer folder and any config file it created — nothing is written to the registry or system directories.
